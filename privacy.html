PostPilot Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

PostPilot is a desktop application that schedules and publishes a user's own videos to social accounts the user connects. This policy explains what information the App handles and where it goes. The short version: everything stays on your computer, and nothing is sent to the App's developer.

1. No developer servers

The App has no backend. It does not send analytics, telemetry, account data, tokens or video files to the developer or to any server operated for the App. The developer cannot see your accounts, your content or your posting history.

3. Third-party services the App talks to

  • Social platforms you connect (TikTok, YouTube, Meta, X, LinkedIn): the App uploads your videos and captions and reads your own post statistics through each platform's official API, under that platform's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude), if you turn on AI captions: a few still frames and a transcript of each video, plus your niche and brand settings, are sent to Anthropic's API to draft titles and captions, under Anthropic's privacy policy. Account tokens are never sent.
  • Speech-model download: on first use the App downloads a speech-recognition model file from Hugging Face so transcription can run on your computer. No audio leaves your computer.

4. TikTok data

Through Login Kit the App receives an access token and the connected account's basic profile. Through the Content Posting API it uploads videos you scheduled, with the privacy level you chose. TikTok data is used only to perform those actions for you. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising.

5. Deleting your data

Disconnecting an account in the App deletes its token from your computer. Deleting the App's folder removes the database, all tokens and all drafted content. You can also revoke the App's access from the platform's own settings, for example TikTok's "Manage app permissions".

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their data.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date at the top shows the current version.
